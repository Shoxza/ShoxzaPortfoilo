# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + vanilla TypeScript, no UI framework (the owner's lecturer discourages bloated framework portfolios, and the owner agrees). Small single-purpose libraries are allowed, for example GSAP or anime.js. Static output hosted on Vercel under the owner's own domain, with the repo at GitHub `Shoxza/ShoxzaPortfoilo`. No backend, database or logins. Anything live (such as a Spotify or Steam status) is an open decision because it may need a serverless function.

## Users

Recruiters and hiring developers looking at a junior front-end / full-stack applicant, usually from a link in an application or CV. They skim fast and need to tell within seconds who this is, what they've built, and how to reach them. A second audience is peers and lecturers.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Shoxza, a web development student at NTNU (Norway) applying for junior front-end / full-stack jobs. It shows personal and school projects in a way that feels like the owner, not a corporate CV, while staying easy for a recruiter to read. Success means a recruiter remembers the site and can find the projects and contact details without hunting.

## Positioning

The owner builds tools for their own hobbies. RaceAssistant is a personal race engineer for sim racing, and Anti F1 Spoiler is a browser extension. The portfolio should feel like it comes from that same person: a casual single-player sim racer (F1, Assetto Corsa Competizione, Le Mans Ultimate), a Red Bull Racing and Max Verstappen fan, and a gamer who hosts board game nights with friends every couple of weeks.

## Operating Context

Read on desktop from an application link, often on a phone too. English only for now. A Norwegian About page may come in a later iteration.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Lightweight and sustainable: small pages, no heavy runtime, free-tier hosting.
- Fancy enough to impress: animation and interaction are welcome, but the site must not feel like Bruno Simon-level overkill.
- One main page with projects that expand in place. No deep menu navigation.
- Contact is links only (email, GitHub, LinkedIn), with no form.
- Undecided: the live Spotify/Steam status strip, project detail pages, and the About page with a travel map (next iteration).

## Brand Commitments

- Headline identity is "Shoxza" (an anime-derived handle; never explain its lore on the site). The owner's real name appears on the About section.
- Intro copy is minimal: "Junior developer". No job-pleading lines such as "looking for a junior role".
- Liked in earlier mockups: a dark asphalt-grey ground (not pure black), a Red Bull Racing blue glow on a few key texts, tyre-compound colours marking project type (red = personal, yellow = school), and small tyre-wheel icons.
- Disliked: anything that looks AI-generated or templated, CV-like sites, austere minimalism, menu-heavy navigation.

## Evidence on Hand

- Projects: RaceAssistant (TypeScript, personal), Anti F1 Spoiler (TypeScript browser extension, personal), Megaminds Poker Dice (MERN, IDG2100 school exam team project). Descriptions come from their READMEs.
- All repos are currently private and will be made public later, so no code links yet.
- No case studies, screenshots or photos exist yet. Never invent case studies, achievements or links.
- doomscroll-assist stays private and is not shown.
- Bachelor thesis in India starting January 2027, and a study visit to Berlin. Reserved for a future About page with a travel map.

## Product Principles

1. Personality first, but readable in one scroll.
2. Show real work honestly. No invented claims.
3. Light and fast beats impressive-but-heavy.
4. One memorable moment beats many effects.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect reduced motion, keep keyboard focus visible, and keep good contrast on the dark ground.
